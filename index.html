ℹ️ About Spello

Multiple users

Each person gets their own profile with separate lists, words and progress. Tap 🔄 in the top bar to switch or add a new user.

For parents

Create spelling lists on the home screen. Tap 📊 to view the current user's full practice and test history.

For kids

Tap any list, choose 📖 Practice or 📝 Test. Hear each word, type it, and get instant feedback.
